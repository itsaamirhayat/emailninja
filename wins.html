The diagnostic thresholds

Four numbers. One method. Check them in your own dashboard right now.

The Inbox Delivery Standard

Every account gets pushed toward a 99/100 sender score on Google Postmaster Tools. Below the 80s, your domain reputation is actively working against you.

The Primary Inbox Threshold

Open rates under 15% almost always mean the Promotions or Spam tab, not the offer. Healthy, well-aligned domains routinely sit in the 25–40% range.

The Cart-Recovery Bar

A well-built abandoned cart flow should recover somewhere between 3–8% of abandoned sessions. If yours is under 2%, the flow is either mistimed or the copy isn't doing its job.

The Spam Filter Ceiling

Spam complaint rate has to stay under the 0.3% hard limit — I build toward keeping every account closer to 0.05% as the real safety margin.
